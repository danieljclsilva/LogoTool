# Synthetic model fixtures — provenance and status

Both files in this directory are **synthetic, procedurally generated test
fixtures**. They are internal-prototype stand-ins only.

| file | shape | vertices | triangles | bytes | sha256 (prefix) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `synthetic-block.glb` | unit box, 6 quad faces | 36 (flat-shaded) | 12 | 1900 | `570d08da…` |
| `synthetic-tetra.glb` | two-tetra abstract silhouette (wide upright lower tetra + smaller inverted upper tetra) | 24 (flat-shaded) | 8 | 1672 | `764cdf40…` |

The two fixtures differ visibly: a symmetric box versus a stacked pair of
tetrahedra with different footprints (lower base radius 0.55, upper base
radius 0.38) and a vertical extent of −0.85…0.95 versus the box's
−0.5…0.5. Regenerate both with:

```sh
node scripts/generate-synthetic-glb.mjs
```

The generator is deterministic (fixed JSON key order, no timestamps):
repeated runs emit byte-identical files.

## Status labels

- **Synthetic** — generated by `scripts/generate-synthetic-glb.mjs`, not
  scanned, modelled, or supplied by any vendor.
- **Uncalibrated** — `calibrationStatus: 'uncalibrated'`; coordinates are
  uncalibrated model-space values for visualization only.
- **Not FlexiTog geometry** — no real product shape, no customer data.
- **No physical dimensions** — nothing in these files is millimetres, and
  nothing here defines placement, sizing, or production measurements.
