Prepare the operation sheet

Write the department's operational instructions and logo dimensions here. Customer notes below are read-only context: they never print on the sheet, only department text prints as operational instructions.

Technique settings (optional — only what you type is printed)

Logo dimensions

One or more measured dimensions per logo: pick the anchor, the artwork edge and the convention for each row. Horizontal (dx) needs a left/right edge, vertical (dy) a bottom/top edge — anything else is refused with the reason shown. Missing anchors stay unset with “setup needed”: the draft still saves, but the sheet prints a draft badge.

Anchor + safe-area setup (one-time product setup)

Click the product photo to create named anchors (inverse-mapped to region millimetres through the evaluated photo mapping — never guessed from photo bounds). Drag markers or use arrow keys (Shift for 10 mm steps) to move them; rename or remove them below. Safe-area corners stage a proposal only: the issued product is never changed here.

Review decision (optional office bookkeeping)

Record the office decision for this customer update. Approval needs the customer confirmation to match this exact update, and every logo to fit its allowed area. This is internal workflow bookkeeping, not a production release.
