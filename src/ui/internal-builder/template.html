Add a product

Add a provisional, uncalibrated product from staged files. One 3D model (.glb), one flat image (PNG, JPEG, WebP), or one assigned side view (front/back/left/right) is enough; each assigned image attaches to its named side only. The product carries no measurements and cannot host placements; missing sides are simply absent, never invented.

Optional per-view labels are attached to the named side only. Only explicitly assigned sides travel with the product; a lone back, left, or right image stays on that side and is never duplicated into front.
